About

I'm an ECE student at the University of Toronto working across two main tracks: firmware for UTAT Space Systems' satellite on-board computer, and IoT/embedded systems through independent and design-team projects. I also serve as VP of Partnerships for UTRA, the university's robotics association. Outside of firmware, I've worked on embedded systems for the Human Powered Vehicle Design Team, and I'm always looking for the next project that mixes hardware, code, and figuring out why something isn't booting.

LoRa Mesh Air Quality Gateway

An ESP32 + cellular gateway for a LoRa mesh air-quality sensor network deployed at Toronto's Meadoway. Built the firmware architecture on FreeRTOS, publishing sensor data to AWS IoT Core over MQTT, with custom battery estimation and deep-sleep power management.

Satellite Firmware — UTAT Space Systems

Bringing up the on-board computer firmware for a custom STM32-based satellite board using Zephyr RTOS and MCUboot, including a signed dual-slot update system and custom flash partition layout.
