Overview

Project management for UTRA's SUMO robotics team, supporting over 100 engineers across hardware, firmware, mechanical design, and competition preparation for autonomous SUMO robots.

Team Management

  • Project planning : coordinated development milestones across hardware, firmware, and mechanical work to prepare teams for the competition.
  • Technical support : led hands-on setup sessions covering Arduino hardware, sensors, wiring, soldering, and robot integration.
  • Training : ran Arduino C++ workshops covering sensor inputs and decision-making for autonomous robot control.
  • CAD training : organized SolidWorks seminars to teach new members the basics of chassis design and robot prototyping.

Competition Preparation

Coordinated team development around the competition deadline, completing key milestones early enough to leave additional time for robot testing and debugging.
