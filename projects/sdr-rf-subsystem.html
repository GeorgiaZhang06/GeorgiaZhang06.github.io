Software Defined Radio: RF Subsystem

Altium, LTSpice, Python, PCB Design
Altium LTSpice Python RF / PCB Design
project photo

Jan. 2026 – Apr. 2026

Overview

  • Designed and fabricated a 3-stage RF front-end PCB in Altium comprising a bandpass filter, dual-channel mixer, and baseband amplifier.
  • Synthesized a 3rd-order LC Butterworth bandpass filter in LTSpice with 50Ω matching; measured band edges at 8.55 MHz and 14.82 MHz via automated oscilloscope FRA sweep.
  • Characterized baseband gain by driving a differential signal into a dual op-amp layout (OPA1642AIDR), achieving 20.32 dB downconversion gain at 3.3 V.
