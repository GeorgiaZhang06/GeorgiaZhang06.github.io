Lighting Control PCB

Embedded systems work for the UofT Human Powered Vehicle Design Team
C++ ESP32 KiCad IMU CAN Bus PCB Design

Sep. 2025 – Present

Overview

Embedded systems development for the University of Toronto Human Powered Vehicle Design Team, developing the vehicle's lighting control hardware and firmware.

Control Board

  • PCB design : designed an ESP32-based lighting control board in KiCad with 12V, 5V, and 3.3V power rails and a CAN bus interface.
  • IMU sensing : integrated an IMU to measure vehicle orientation and detect rollover conditions.
  • Lighting control : implemented C++ firmware to trigger the vehicle's warning lighting when rollover thresholds are exceeded.
  • Vehicle communication : added CAN bus communication for interfacing the lighting controller with other vehicle control boards.

System Operation

The ESP32 continuously reads vehicle orientation from the IMU and compares it against defined rollover thresholds. When the vehicle exceeds these limits, the firmware activates the warning lighting to indicate a rollover condition.
