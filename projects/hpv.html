Overview

Embedded systems work for the University of Toronto's Human Powered Vehicle Design Team, covering both the control PCB hardware and the firmware that runs on it.

  • Designed an ESP32-based control PCB in KiCad with a 3-rail (12V / 5V / 3.3V) power system, a CAN bus node, and USB-C input.
