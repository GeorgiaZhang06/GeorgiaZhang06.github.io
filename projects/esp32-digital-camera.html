ESP32 Digital Camera

Standalone digital camera built around an ESP32
C++ ESP32 Camera WiFi Soldering Hardware

Jul. 2026 - Aug. 2026

Overview

Built a standalone digital camera using an ESP32 camera module, with physical shutter and flash controls. The project combined embedded C++ firmware with hands-on hardware assembly and a custom enclosure.

Firmware

  • Image capture : configured the ESP32 camera to capture JPEG images and store the latest frame in memory.
  • Physical controls : implemented shutter and flash buttons with debouncing and press detection.
  • WiFi : connected the camera to WiFi and hosted a camera server for remote viewing.

Hardware Build

  • Assembly : soldered the shutter button, flash button, and LED connections to the camera hardware.
  • Enclosure : built a custom outer casing to house the camera and physical controls.
